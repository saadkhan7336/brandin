# Brandly — Complete Product Knowledge Base & Customer Support Manual

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Overview
**Brandly** is a premier AI-powered brand–influencer matchmaking, campaign collaboration, and secure escrow settlement platform. Known as *"the pulse of creator commerce"*, Brandly streamlines end-to-end creator marketing by connecting high-growth businesses with verified creators across YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Account Types

### A. Brands & Marketing Teams
- **Purpose**: Scout verified influencers, automate campaign workflows, manage deliverables, hold funds in secure escrow, and track campaign ROI.
- **Team Roles**:
  - **Admin**: Full control over campaigns, team billing, Stripe escrow payments, and contracts.
  - **Editor**: Can create/edit campaign briefs, communicate with creators, and audit deliverables.
  - **Viewer**: Read-only access to campaign analytics, match scores, and progress boards.

### B. Influencers & Digital Creators
- **Purpose**: Connect authentic social channels via OAuth, discover campaigns matched by AI, collaborate directly in dedicated workspaces, submit milestone deliverables, and receive guaranteed payouts via Stripe Connect.
- **Eligible Niches**: Fashion, Beauty, Tech & Gadgets, Gaming, Fitness & Health, Lifestyle, Food, Travel, Business/Finance, and Education.

## 3. Core Features & Capabilities

### 3.1 AI Matching & Scoring Algorithm
- **How it works**: Analyzes real-time social metrics (follower count, engagement rates, audience demographics, content sentiment, and historical completion rates) against brand campaign briefs.
- **Match Score (0–100%)**:
  - **90%+ (Exceptional Fit)**: Highly aligned audience demographics, peak engagement, and niche relevance.
  - **75%–89% (Strong Fit)**: High potential with strong category overlap.
  - **Below 75%**: Broad match or exploratory collaboration.

### 3.2 OAuth Social Account Verification
- Creators link their **YouTube**, **Instagram**, and **TikTok** accounts securely using official OAuth 2.0 protocols.
- **Data Verified**: Live subscriber/follower counts, median view counts, average engagement rates, and recent video performance.
- **Privacy & Security**: Brandly **never** requests or stores social media passwords.

### 3.3 Workspace Collaboration & Chat
- Multi-room, real-time messaging with live typing indicators and file sharing (scripts, video previews, contracts).
- Milestone checklists embedded directly within the chat workspace.

## 4. Financial Architecture: Escrow & Payouts

### 4.1 The Escrow Model (Powered by Stripe)
To ensure 100% financial security for both parties:
1. **Campaign Funding**: When a brand hires an influencer or initiates a milestone, the agreed budget is placed into Stripe Escrow.
2. **Work in Progress**: The creator receives a guarantee that funds are locked and reserved.
3. **Audit & Review**: The creator submits the deliverable link/file. The brand has up to **7 days** to review, request revisions, or approve.
4. **Instant Release**: Upon brand approval, funds are immediately released from escrow to the creator's Stripe Connect balance.

### 4.2 Payouts & Stripe Connect
- **Supported Methods**: Direct Bank Deposit, Debit Card Transfers.
- **KYC Requirement**: Creators must complete standard Stripe KYC (identity & bank verification).
- **Processing Time**: Payouts typically reflect within **24 hours** after release from escrow.
- **Currency Support**: Multi-currency support (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, PKR, and more depending on region).

### 4.3 Platform Fees & Pricing
- **For Creators**: **100% Free to join**, apply to campaigns, and receive payments.
- **For Brands**: Standard transparent platform service fee applied during campaign funding to cover AI matching, milestone auditing, and escrow protection.

## 5. Dispute Resolution & Cancellation Policy

### 5.1 Dispute Workflow
1. **Triggering a Dispute**: Either party can open a support ticket with the **Campaign ID** if deadlines are missed, deliverables violate the agreed brief, or communication fails.
2. **Evidence Collection**: Brandly moderators review chat logs, milestone timestamps, brief requirements, and uploaded deliverables.
3. **Resolution Window**: Disputes are investigated and resolved within **3 to 5 business days**.
4. **Outcomes**: Full escrow refund to the brand, full release to the creator, or a fair split based on completed milestones.

### 5.2 Campaign Cancellations
- **Before Work Starts**: 100% full escrow refund back to the brand's payment method.
- **In Progress**: Mutual cancellation required or resolved via mediation.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: How do I get started as a Brand?
**Ans**: Register an account, set up your brand profile, click **"Create Campaign"**, write your brief with deliverables and budget, fund the Stripe escrow, and browse AI-recommended creators or accept applications.

#### Q2: How do I get started as a Creator?
**Ans**: Sign up as an influencer, complete your profile bio, connect your active YouTube/Instagram/TikTok accounts via OAuth, and apply to campaigns in the **Discovery** feed.

#### Q3: What happens if an influencer misses a campaign deadline?
**Ans**: Brands can send a reminder or request a deadline extension. If the creator remains unresponsive, the brand can open a dispute to cancel the milestone and receive a full escrow refund.

#### Q4: Are my social media credentials safe?
**Ans**: Yes. Brandly only uses official OAuth tokens to read verified metrics and never accesses your private passwords or direct messages.

#### Q5: How can I reach human support?
**Ans**: Email us at `support@brandly.com` or use our Contact Form at `/contact`. Our support team responds within 24 hours.
